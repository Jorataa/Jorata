# Artwork generation notes

Built-in image generation produced nine independent raster originals; local deterministic export generated crops, WebP encodings, SVG artwork, sprite strip and preview compositions. No third-party artwork or component code was copied.

## Final prompt set / art directions
Every raster prompt specified original premium fine-square-pixel indie art, navy #101B2B, deep blue #1C2D40, slate #314155, sparse mint #5FE1C3, warm gold #F4C978; no text, logos, UI, frames or collages.

1. Desktop hero, 1536×1024: Midnight District rooftop city at blue hour; left 45% dark open sky for headings, right stepped tower and greenhouse with warm windows, moon upper right, foreground parapet.
2. Surgery cover, 1536×1024: covered patient centered under surgical lamp, two calm masked surgeons, mint ECG monitor, blue shadows, cinematic clean operating room; no gore or blood.
3. Boxing cover, 1536×1024: two adult athletes in defensive guard, teal and amber/coral gloves and shorts, warm overhead arena light, navy audience, believable ring geometry; no impact or blood.
4. Rooftop Escape cover, 1536×1024: charcoal runner with small mint accent leaping roof gap, centered coherent silhouette, golden moon behind, layered night city and warm window light.
5. Far skyline transparent plate, 1536×1024: continuous distant buildings along lower 32% at left, rising toward right, low contrast and sparse lit windows, no sky or ground plane.
6. Midground transparent plate, 1536×1024: cozy greenhouse apartment roof architecture on right, low building line on left, clear upper-left alpha, navy masonry and gold window light.
7. Foreground transparent plate, 1536×1024: dark rooftop terrace and parapet only along lower third, planters, pipe, wall lamp and antenna at edges, upper canvas transparent.
8. Mobile hero, 1024×1536: separate tall rooftop-city composition, top 45% quiet navy sky for heading and CTA, architecture and greenhouse below, small warm moon at upper-right below text zone.
9. Boxing mobile, 1024×1536: separately composed portrait confrontation with both faces and raised gloves inside middle width, upper-thigh crop, full-bleed arena lighting, no padded borders.

The parallax plates are independent originals composed with a vector-free opaque sky base; they were never extracted as unrelated slices from the desktop hero. Responsive cover variants are deliberate crops of each corresponding single artwork, with the Boxing portrait generated independently where a crop lost its subjects.
